Accessibility
Varallisuuspolku's accessibility statement
Open the planning tool

Varallisuuspolku is a private, free web service that the accessibility requirements of the Finnish Act on the Provision of Digital Services do not bind. This statement has nevertheless been drawn up voluntarily, because financial planning should be within as many people's reach as possible. The aim is WCAG 2.1 AA level in the core functions. The service hasn't been audited externally — the statement is based on self-assessment. Updated 14 July 2026.

What works

Keyboard useAll form fields, toggles and buttons are keyboard-operable, and focus is clearly visible. Shortcuts: F opens the drawing board, Esc closes, Ctrl+Z undoes.
Alternative to draggingEvery change you can make by dragging on the chart can also be made with form fields: the basics, event ages and amounts (the On the timeline list) and the retirement settings.
Alternative to the chartThe annual table (below the chart) presents the same information as a year-by-year text table and exports it as CSV. Key results are also shown as text cards above the chart.
Screen readerCharts have text equivalents (ARIA), changes are announced in an aria-live region and modals have proper roles. As a whole, though, drag-based planning is smoothest through the form fields and the annual table.
Reduced motionThe operating system's reduce motion setting (prefers-reduced-motion) removes animations and smooth scrolling.
No time limitsThe service has no time limits, no auto-dismissing notifications and no flashing content. Your plan is saved automatically.
ScalingThe page works with browser zoom and on mobile devices. The fullest experience is on a large screen.

Known shortcomings

Dragging the chartGrabbing and dragging the curve isn't possible with the keyboard alone. The same result can be achieved with the form fields, but direct curve editing relies on a pointing device.
Dense informationThe chart's tooltips and the drawing board's meters are visually dense; with a screen reader the same information is available from the annual table and the result cards, but not at the same granularity.
ContrastThe dark theme's faintest helper texts don't reach AA contrast everywhere. They are fixed as the spots are identified.

Feedback

If you hit a barrier this statement doesn't recognise, please tell us: a GitHub issue is the fastest route. Accessibility improvements get high priority.
