Assumptions and versioning

Tax rulesFinnish capital income taxation, tax year 2026 parameters: 30 % of annual gains up to 30 000 €, 34 % above that. Deemed acquisition cost 40 % (ownership ≥ 10 y) / 20 %.
Transfer taxOn buying a home 1.5 % (housing company share) and a cottage 3 % (real property) of the price, paid from own funds at the time of purchase; an event-specific percentage overrides the default (detached house 3 %). Already-owned assets have no purchase moment.
Return assumptionsStocks 7 %, bonds 3 %, cash 1.5 % per year (nominal). Editable in Pro mode.
Inflation assumption2 %/yr (editable). Inflation adjustment with the exact Fisher formula (1+r)/(1+i).
Monte Carlo5 000 paths, fixed per-path seeds (CRN) — the same result on every run, deltas comparable.
Verified4 Sep 2026 — tax rules and assumptions are reviewed at least once per tax year. Change 4 Sep 2026: the main line is the median path — the chart's main line and the figures solved on the expected path (savings, sustainable income, retirement age without a confidence level) now follow the median approximation of returns (1+μ)·e−σ²/2, which matches the middle path of the Monte Carlo distribution; the earlier arithmetic expected-value path overestimated typical final wealth by 20–150 % (volatility drag). Success probability, the fan and figures solved at a confidence level did not change. Change 4 Sep 2026: the statutory pension follows the retirement age — the pension field is the estimate at pension age assuming work continues until then; if you retire earlier, the calculation reduces the pension by the accrual that ends (P × (retirement age − 23) / (pension age − 23)). Previously the pension was constant regardless of retirement age, which overestimated an early retiree's pension by up to 40 %. The option "Pension does not depend on retirement age" bypasses the adjustment. Earlier 2 Sep 2026: Change 2 Sep 2026: one-off expenses funded from investments and loan service exceeding savings realise capital gains tax under the same rule as retirement withdrawals (previously untaxed — same portfolio, same sale, same rule). Change 2 Sep 2026: transfer tax on buying a home (1.5 %) and a cottage (3 %). Change 2 Sep 2026: an optional purchase price in the taxable sale of an already owned asset — tax on the actual gain, not always on the deemed share. Change 2 Sep 2026: the pension field asks for the take-home (net) estimate — the pension record shows gross.

Monthly factor k = 1.071/12·d, where d = e−σ²/24 is the median path's volatility correction (100 % stocks: σ = 16 %, d ≈ 0.99893) — the main line follows the typical, i.e. median, development rather than the arithmetic expected value (see simplifications). The savings and withdrawal phase formulas correspond to the spreadsheet FV function with monthly rates.

Worked tax examples computed step by step

Capital gains tax and deemed acquisition cost (asset sales)

Asunto ostetaan 220 000 €:lla ja myydään 10 vuoden kuluttua, arvonnousu 2 %/v. Ei merkitty verovapaaksi omaksi asunnoksi.

Sale price220 000 € · 1,0210 = 268 178,77 €
Actual gain268 178,77 − 220 000 = 48 178,77 €
Deemed acquisition cost (ownership ≥ 10 y)taxable at most 60 % of the sale price = 160 907.26 € → the actual gain is smaller, so 48 178.77 € is taxed
Tax by bracket30 000 · 30 % + 18 178,77 · 34 % = 9 000,00 + 6 180,78 = 15 180,78 €
Engine result15 180,78 € — matches

Retirement withdrawal: gross → net (profit-share tax)

Nostoista verotetaan vain voiton osuus. Esimerkki: salkun arvo 500 000 €, josta hankintahintaa 250 000 € (voitto-osuus 50 %), ja eläkeläinen tarvitsee 2 000 € käteen.

Gross saleneed / (1 − profit share · tax) = 2 000 / (1 − 0.5 · 0.30) = 2 352,94 €
Taxprofit share · gross · 30 % = 0.5 · 2 352.94 · 0.30 = 352,94 €
In hand2 352.94 − 352.94 = 2 000.00 € — exactly the need

Engine seuraa salkun hankintahintaa kuukausittain: säästöt kasvattavat sitä, myynnit pienentävät suhteessa, ja tuotto kasvattaa vain arvoa. Vuotuiset realisoidut voitot lasketaan yhteen 30 000 €:n verorajaa varten, ja laskuri nollaa ne kalenterivuosittain.

The dividend tax drag in a book-entry account

Suorien osakkeiden osingoista 85 % on veronalaista pääomatuloa. Jos osinkotuotoksi asetetaan 3,5 %/v, arvo-osuustilillä vuosituotosta vähenee 3,5 % · 85 % · 30 % ≈ 0,89 prosenttiyksikköä osakepainon osalta. Osakesäästötilillä ja vakuutuskuoressa osingot kertyvät verotta — tämä ero on tilivertailun ydin.

Monte Carlo: repeatability and properties

Onnistumistodennäköisyys lasketaan tuhansista satunnaisista markkinapoluista. Satunnaisuus on siemennettyä (polku i saa aina saman shokkijonon), joten sama suunnitelma antaa täsmälleen saman tuloksen joka ajolla ja eri suunnitelmien vertailut ovat reiluja — molemmat kokevat samat markkinahistoriat. Automaattiset testit varmistavat lisäksi, että viuhkan alaraja (P10) ei koskaan ylitä ylärajaa (P90), että viuhka alkaa alkupääomasta ja että ehtymiskäyrän loppuarvo on täsmälleen 1 − onnistumistodennäköisyys.

Esimerkki: oletussuunnitelman onnistumistodennäköisyys 99,0 % — bitilleen sama jokaisella ajolla ja jokaisella laitteella.

Known simplifications what the model does NOT account for

Jokainen malli yksinkertaistaa. Nämä ovat tiedossa olevat poikkeamat todellisuudesta — laskelma on suuntaa antava havainnollistus, ei veroilmoitus:

Equity savings account deposit capThe 100 000 € cap isn't modelled in the calculation (the interface warns when it's exceeded).
Annual tax ledgerRetirement withdrawals track the gains realised during the year (the 30/34 % threshold), but a sale of property is taxed as its own event with its own threshold — withdrawals and sales in the same year do not share a single 30,000 € threshold. The model understates tax when a large sale and large withdrawals fall in the same year.
Loss deductionNot modelled — only gains are taxed; deductions for loss years aren't credited.
Deemed acquisition cost on withdrawalsPro mode's deemed-cost rule for monthly retirement withdrawals estimates the holding period from the start of the plan, not per lot.
Holdings (current state)An already-owned home or cottage is entered as its current value and remaining loan — the purchase price is optional: without it, the taxable share of a taxable sale is computed with the deemed acquisition cost (20 % / 40 % depending on holding period; an optional purchase year extends the holding period, otherwise it counts from now), with it from the actual gain (the deemed cost still caps it). A primary home is tax-free on sale by default.
Main line = median pathThe chart's main line follows the median approximation of returns: monthly return (1+μ)1/12·e−σ²/24, where σ is the portfolio's annual volatility. It matches the middle path (P50) of the Monte Carlo distribution to within about ±10 %. An arithmetic expected-value path would overestimate the typical outcome, because volatility eats geometric growth (volatility drag ≈ σ²/2 ≈ 1–2 percentage points per year at a high stock weight). The deterministic solvers (savings, sustainable income, retirement age without a confidence level) use the same path. Monte Carlo simulates with the arithmetic expected return plus random shocks, so the drag is fully present there — the fan (P10–P90), the success probability and the confidence levels do not depend on the choice of main line. Change 4 Sep 2026 (previously the main line was the arithmetic expected-value path).
Earnings-related pensionThe user's own estimate as take-home monthly income at pension age (the Finnish Centre for Pensions' estimate assumes work continues until then) — not an official calculation. If you retire before the pension age, the pension is reduced by the accrual that ends using the accrual approximation P × (retirement age − 23) / (pension age − 23), which needs no salary data (career start at 23 is a constant; actual accrual depends on earnings). No deferral increase (0.4 %/month) is added and the life expectancy coefficient is assumed to be included in the estimate. Income tax on the pension, the national pension and the guarantee pension are not modelled.
Asset class correlationThe basic version's uncertainty fan assumes full correlation (a conservative simplification — the real diversification benefit is larger). In Pro mode you can set the correlations yourself. The effect is small: with a realistic stock–bond correlation (0.1) the success probability changes by at most 3–5 percentage points, and only for balanced portfolios whose result is near 50 % (measured 4 Sep 2026 with the example plans) — which is why the basic version keeps the conservative assumption.
Return distributionMonthly returns from a normal distribution with no autocorrelation (Pro: fat-tailed Student's t). Real markets can deviate from this.
InflationA constant assumption for the whole plan — real inflation varies.
Changes to tax rulesThe calculation applies today's rules across the whole lifecycle — future law changes aren't predicted.

Check it yourself

Kaikki tämän sivun tarkistukset — ja kymmeniä muita — ajetaan automaattisesti jokaisen koodimuutoksen yhteydessä. Voit toistaa ne itse:

  1. Get the source code: github.com/olavikurola/varallisuuspolku
  2. Run the unit tests: node testit/laskenta.test.js — no dependencies, plain Node.js is enough.
  3. Compare for yourself: the app's annual table (below the chart) shows the numbers year by year and exports them as CSV to a spreadsheet, where you can build your own comparison.

Testit kattavat mm. korkolaskuidentiteetit, verolaskennan, annuiteettilainat, Monte Carlon determinismin, käänteisratkaisijat (bisektio), tavoitepisteet, varmuustasot, perhelaskennan koherenssin ja Pro-tilan oletusarvojen bittitason yhtäpitävyyden perustilan kanssa.

Laskelma on suuntaa antava havainnollistus, ei sijoitusneuvontaa eikä veroneuvontaa. Historiallinen tuotto ei ole tae tulevasta. Takaisin suunnittelutyökaluun · Accessibility
